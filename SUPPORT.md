# XperSlot support and issue reporting

## Before reporting

Check the [current Android release status](RELEASE_STATUS.md) first. The XperSlot Customer and Business apps are the current product pair. APKs are not available for public download until a release is published with both complete APK attachments.

For a reproducible issue, include:
- App: XperSlot Customer or XperSlot Business.
- App version and Android version, if applicable.
- Device manufacturer and model, if relevant.
- Steps to reproduce the issue.
- Expected behavior and actual behavior.
- A redacted screenshot or log only when it helps explain the problem.

## Do not include

Do not post passwords, one-time codes, access tokens, private business or customer data, payment details, private messages, or unredacted logs.

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/wondxpt/XperSlot-Downloads/issues) for product feedback and non-sensitive support requests. For suspected security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
